Justice Pro — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

Thank you for using Justice Pro. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Justice Pro may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile information (optional)
  • Identity details provided during account creation

1.2 Usage Data

  • App interactions
  • Feature usage analytics
  • Device information (model, OS version)

1.3 Communication Data

  • Chat messages between clients and lawyers
  • Images, documents, and files shared inside chat
  • Timestamps and message metadata

1.4 Payment Information (if applicable)

Transaction details for deposits and withdrawals, billing information, and wallet balance. We do not store credit/debit card details — payments are processed through trusted third-party providers such as Stripe.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable chat, messaging, and communication features
  • Process deposits, withdrawals, and payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Maintain safety, security, and fraud prevention

3. Data Storage & Security

We take strong measures to secure your data:

  • All data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage.
  • Communication is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS.
  • Access permissions are controlled using Firebase Security Rules.

We do not share your personal information with unauthorized third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share data only in the following cases:

  • Legal requirements: To comply with law, government requests, or court orders.
  • Trusted providers: Such as Firebase, Google Services, or Stripe for payments.
  • With your consent: If you grant permission for specific features.

We never sell your personal data.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Justice Pro does not use browser cookies. Analytics and crash-reporting tools may collect anonymous usage data to improve functionality.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Request clarification about how your data is used

To request data removal or account deletion, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s Privacy

Justice Pro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
